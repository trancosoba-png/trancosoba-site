# Condomínio Icatú in Trancoso: houses, location and accommodation profile

The area can interest mainly families, couples and small groups willing to organize their journeys during the trip.

Icatú is a condominium with a compact, residential profile, offering simpler and more accessible accommodation options compared to high-standard areas such as Altos de Trancoso, Pedro Grande and Terravista.

The properties advertised in the area do not follow a single standard. Size, number of suites, social areas, services and access conditions depend on the chosen house. That is why the property's exact location is as important as the condominium's name at booking time.

## What staying at Condomínio Icatú is like

Icatú has a residential profile and simpler structure, with accommodation in houses and less tourist circulation than in the immediate surroundings of the Quadrado. This characteristic can favor those who want to use the property as a base for the trip, with more space for socializing and a routine less tied to the movement of the center.

The experience, however, varies considerably between properties. Some houses may offer an equipped kitchen, pool, garden, parking or services during the stay; others have a simpler structure. These items should not be considered general characteristics of Icatú without checking the listing and the accommodation contract.

Before booking, check:

- quantity and distribution of the suites;
- bed types and maximum capacity;
- structure of the kitchen and the social areas;
- presence of a pool, garden or other outdoor spaces;
- parking and access conditions;
- services included and charged separately;
- rules for children, pets and visitors;
- exact location of the property.

## House sizes and guest profile

There are houses from two suites in Icatú, as well as larger properties for families and groups.

A two-suite house can serve couples traveling together or small families who want separate bedrooms. For larger families and groups, it is necessary to assess not only the number of bedrooms, but also the quantity of beds, bathrooms, common areas and parking spaces.

The capacity declared by the property's manager should be respected. It is also worth observing whether the house's configuration offers the privacy the group expects, especially when there are children, elderly people or people with specific mobility needs.

## Location, access and getting around

Icatú is farther from the Quadrado than João Vieira and Colina. It is also more than 2 km from the beach and is not a beachfront condominium. For this reason, getting around by car is usually more practical.

It is not advisable to take a single distance as a reference for all houses. The route can change according to the property, the available road and the chosen destination. Going to the Quadrado, for example, involves different logistics than heading to the beaches or to other areas of Trancoso.

Beyond the mileage, consider:

- conditions of the route;
- travel time during busy periods;
- ease of finding parking;
- getting around at night;
- the beach you intend to visit;
- cost of taxis, transfers or drivers.

Before closing the accommodation, request the house's exact location and simulate the journeys that will be part of the routine. This check helps avoid a choice based only on descriptions such as "near the center" or "a few kilometers from the beach".

### Is a car necessary?

A car tends to make the stay easier for those who intend to move between the Quadrado, beaches, markets and restaurants. This does not mean it is mandatory on every trip, but relying only on walking can limit the itinerary, especially at night, with children or when carrying beach gear.

Those who do not intend to drive should check in advance the availability and cost of taxis, transfers or drivers. Transportation spending needs to enter the comparison between different accommodations, especially when the savings on the daily rate depend on staying farther from the frequented places.

It is also important to confirm whether the house offers parking and whether the access is suitable for the vehicle that will be used.

## Icatú's value for money

Icatú is one of the alternatives with the best value for money for those who want to spend less on accommodation in Trancoso. Its average standard and structure tend to be simpler than those found in areas such as Altos de Trancoso, Pedro Grande and Terravista.

Even so, the conditions of each property and the prices charged need to be compared for the travel period. Rates vary according to:

- season and travel dates;
- number of suites;
- property capacity;
- structure and standard of the house;
- included services;
- length of stay;
- how far in advance the booking is made;
- holidays and local events.

A lower daily rate does not necessarily represent a lower total cost. Transportation, extra services and travel time are also part of the bill. For a fair comparison, use properties with similar capacity, structure and stay period.

## Icatú, João Vieira or Rua dos Macacos?

Comparisons with other areas help understand Icatú's profile, but do not replace the analysis of each house's address.

João Vieira is usually sought by those who want to stay in a residential area with more convenient access to the Quadrado. In some stretches, walking to the Quadrado is feasible, although this depends on the exact location of the house.

Icatú is farther from the Quadrado than João Vieira, and getting around by car is usually more practical. The area tends to enter the research of those who prioritize more accessible accommodation and accept depending on transportation for part of the itinerary.

In practical terms, compare:

- exact address of the house;
- real possibility of walking to the Quadrado;
- ease of car access;
- parking;
- total cost of accommodation and transportation.

### Icatú or Rua dos Macacos

Rua dos Macacos corresponds to a residential, leafy area, farther from the Quadrado. A car is usually important, although the distance and ease of access can vary from one property to another.

Trancoso Eco Residence and La Dolce Vita Trancoso are developments associated with the Rua dos Macacos area. Specific operational data for each should be confirmed before booking.

In Icatú and Rua dos Macacos, the area's name offers only an initial orientation. To choose between the two, it is safer to compare the location, route, structure and services of specific properties.

## Who Icatú can make sense for

The area's profile can match:

- couples who want to stay in a house and do not prioritize immediate proximity to the Quadrado;
- families who value separate bedrooms, a kitchen and social areas;
- small groups interested in sharing a property;
- travelers willing to use a car or hire transportation;
- people who prefer to assess space and structure before the possibility of doing everything on foot;
- guests looking for a more accessible alternative in Trancoso.

On the other hand, those who want to go out at night without depending on transportation, walk frequently to the Quadrado or stay directly in front of the beach should compare Icatú with areas closer to those points.

## What to check before booking

The assessment should be made property by property. Ask the house's manager for objective and, when possible, recent information:

1. Address or exact location on the map.
2. Best access route.
3. Estimated time to the Quadrado and the beaches of interest.
4. Real quantity of suites, beds and bathrooms.
5. Maximum permitted capacity.
6. Structure of the kitchen and outdoor spaces.
7. Parking and route characteristics.
8. Services included in the daily rate.
9. Cancellation, visitor and pet rules.
10. Additional costs for cleaning, energy, staff or transportation.

Updated photos and detailed descriptions help, but do not replace the direct confirmation of the points that influence the group's routine.
