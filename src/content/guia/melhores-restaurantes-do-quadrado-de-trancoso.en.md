# Best restaurants on Trancoso's Quadrado: where to eat, what to order and which one suits each occasion

Among historic little houses, hidden gardens, outdoor tables and façades lit up at night, there are restaurants of Bahian cuisine, fish and seafood, Italian cooking, parrilla, sushi, contemporary proposals, cafés and more casual meals.

There is not, however, a single restaurant that suits every moment. A traditional moqueca calls for one choice; a dinner for two, another; a pizza night with the family, yet another. It also matters whether you are looking for lunch, coffee, drinks or a quick meal before walking around the village.

This guide considers establishments located on Trancoso's Quadrado or in its immediate core. Beach restaurants, Terravista, Rio da Barra, Caraíva, Arraial d'Ajuda and distant areas are not part of this selection.

> **Editorial criterion:** hours, menus, prices and operating formats can change throughout the year. Confirm directly with the establishment, especially in the low season, on holidays and on busy dates.

## Why eat on Trancoso's Quadrado?

The Quadrado is not just Trancoso's historic square. It also concentrates a very diverse gastronomic scene, set in old little houses, gardens, inns and spaces geared toward the evening.

Within an area that can be covered on foot, you will find:

- traditional Bahian cuisine;
- moquecas, fish and seafood;
- contemporary Brazilian cooking;
- Italian restaurants, pizzas and pasta;
- parrillas and steakhouses;
- sushi and Japanese preparations;
- cafés, breads, cakes and snacks;
- sweet and savory tapiocas;
- lunch by weight or buffet, depending on the operation;
- bars with drinks and gastronomy.

At night, the colorful façades, the low lighting and the tables set out in front of the little houses help create the setting for which the Quadrado became known. The experience, however, changes with the season: holidays, public holidays, January, Carnival and New Year's Eve usually bring more movement.

## Classics and Bahian food

### Capim Santo

Capim Santo occupies an important position in Trancoso's gastronomic history. With origins in the 1980s and a connection to chef Morena Leite, the restaurant helped project a creative Brazilian cuisine based on regional ingredients.

The garden is part of the experience. The proposal is not limited to reproducing traditional recipes: it works Brazilian and Bahian references with its own reading.

On the menu, it is worth looking for dishes with regional ingredients, fish and seafood, as well as preparations linked to lemongrass (capim-santo), an element present in the house's historic identity. The selection of dishes should be checked on the current menu.

It mainly suits dinner for two, special occasions and travelers interested in Trancoso's gastronomic history.

### Silvana & Cia

Silvana & Cia is one of the Quadrado's oldest restaurants and a reference for those seeking traditional-style Bahian cuisine.

Moquecas, fish and seafood are linked to the house's trajectory. The outdoor tables and the family character help form an experience closer to the local tradition than to an international cuisine.

It is an especially pertinent choice for a first trip to Trancoso, for families and for those who want to try a moqueca. As dishes can vary, it is worth checking the types of fish, shrimp and side dishes available on the day.

### O Cacau

Near the church, O Cacau combines Brazilian and Bahian cuisine with an environment surrounded by vegetation.

Fish, seafood and moquecas are among the house's references. The shrimp bobó also appears historically among the known dishes, but its presence needs to be verified on the current menu.

The house suits dinner for two and those who want Bahian food in a more secluded environment thanks to the vegetation. For sharing, it is worth looking at the moquecas, starters and fish preparations offered on the day.

## Fish, Mediterranean cuisine and contemporary proposals

### Favoritto Trancoso

A TrancosoBA partner, Favoritto is located on the Quadrado and operates mainly at dinner, with outdoor tables. The kitchen follows a Mediterranean profile, with a relevant presence of fish and seafood.

It is an option for those who intend to make dinner the central program of the evening, whether as a couple or in a group. On the menu, it is worth looking at starters to share, fish, shrimp, rice dishes, pasta and drinks, according to the current selection.

In busier periods, a reservation may be advisable, especially for groups or for those who want a specific time.

### El Gordo

El Gordo brings together a restaurant and an inn in a position near the lookout, at the end of the Quadrado. The cuisine has a signature profile, with Portuguese and international influences.

The location is an important part of the experience. Some tables or areas may offer a view, but this should not be interpreted as a guaranteed feature throughout the restaurant.

Risottos, fish and other signature preparations appear among the house's references. The menu and the availability of dishes should be checked directly.

It is a choice associated with dinners for two, special occasions and nights when atmosphere and location carry as much weight as the cuisine.

### Flô Trancoso

Flô presents a contemporary cuisine on the Quadrado, with Mediterranean references, the presence of seafood and signature dishes.

The proposal serves those looking for an elaborate dinner but who do not necessarily want traditional Bahian food or Italian cuisine. It is more suitable for couples, special occasions and travelers interested in contemporary preparations.

Specific suggestions vary according to the current menu.

### Doma

Doma is one of the gastronomic references of the Quadrado and the region. As the name, menu, prices and operating format can change, these details should be checked directly before the visit.

### UXUA

The gastronomic operation linked to UXUA Casa Hotel brings together local ingredients and a sophisticated proposal integrated into the historic context of the Quadrado.

The experience is associated with special occasions, dinner for two and a high standard of service. The name used for each gastronomic space and the current menu should be checked directly.

## Italian, pizzas and variety for sharing

### Gino Trancoso

Gino is one of the Italian cuisine options on the Quadrado. Pasta and preparations with seafood are part of its proposal, in an environment geared mainly to dinner.

It is worth looking for pasta, Italian starters, desserts and combinations with fish, according to the current selection. When fresh pasta is available, availability should also be confirmed on the menu.

It is a versatile choice for couples, families or groups who want to vary from Bahian cuisine.

### Maritaca

Maritaca is part of Trancoso's gastronomic memory as a pizzeria and Italian restaurant. Pizzas, pasta and risottos appear historically associated with the operation.

The casual profile and the possibility of sharing make the house a practical alternative for families and groups. Flavors, pizza sizes and other dishes should be checked on the current menu.

### Allegria Trancoso

A TrancosoBA partner linked to the Favoritto Group, Allegria occupies an open-air garden on the Quadrado. Its proposal brings together different influences, with Italian references, naturally leavened pizza and the presence of Japanese cuisine or sushi in recent information.

This does not mean that all fronts are available every day or throughout the year. The offer of pizzas, pasta, Japanese preparations and starters to share should be checked according to the current operation.

The variety can work well for groups with different preferences and for those looking for dinner in a garden setting.

## Meats, parrilla and Japanese cuisine

### Bonitto Trancoso

A TrancosoBA partner linked to the Favoritto Group, Bonitto works with meats, parrilla and contemporary cuisine.

The house offers an alternative in a region where moquecas, fish and shrimp usually take up much of the attention. It is indicated for those who prefer meats, want to vary from seafood or are looking for a group dinner.

On the menu, it is worth looking at cuts, parrilla preparations, side dishes, starters and drinks. The specific offer varies according to the current menu.

### Chimichurri

Chimichurri is an option focused on meats, with a steakhouse or parrilla profile and Argentine influence.

Cuts, doneness, sauces, garnishes and side dishes need to be checked on the current menu.

The proposal suits group dinners and travelers who want to intersperse Bahian cuisine with a meal centered on meats.

### Sushi do Quadrado

Sushi do Quadrado serves those who want to vary from moquecas, pasta, meats and Brazilian or Mediterranean preparations with seafood.

The menu should be checked directly, without presuming combinations, fish species, sizes or available techniques.

It can work for dinner for two, a lighter meal or sharing different Japanese preparations.

## Lunch, casual food and traditional restaurants

### Rabanete

Rabanete occupies a different place among the Quadrado's restaurants. Its proposal is historically linked to lunch, with food by weight or buffet, according to the current format, and local references to the use of a wood-fired stove.

Compared with the Quadrado's more sophisticated dinners, it is usually remembered as a casual alternative. The main advantage lies in the possibility of assembling the plate according to hunger and the options offered on the day.

It is a practical choice for lunch, families, variety and greater control over the size of the meal. The service format should be verified before the visit.

### Delícia do Quadrado

Delícia do Quadrado is a real reference on the Quadrado, with a simple and traditional profile and references to Brazilian food, seafood and dinner.

Menu, hours and operating format can change and should be checked directly.

### Ali na Janete

Ali na Janete appears associated with Brazilian food, seafood and drinks, operating at lunch or dinner depending on the phase and the season.

The proposal is a casual meal. Hours, menu and operation should be verified before the visit.

### Cantinho Doce

Cantinho Doce is a traditional Trancoso restaurant. There are historical references to shrimp dishes, seafood, desserts and sweets, with no guarantee that all remain available.

It is an address to consider for its traditional character. The operation and the current menu should be checked directly.

## Cafés, snacks, drinks and gastronomic spaces

Tasca and Muru are among the operations present in that space. Menus, hours and forms of service can vary between the establishments.

The evaluation of the whole should also not be confused with the experience of each operation installed on the site.

### Tasca

Tasca is a Portuguese-style restaurant installed in the space associated with the former Na Praça.

Menu, hours and opening should be checked directly before the visit.

### Muru

Muru is one of the operations present in the space associated with the former Na Praça.

Menu, hours, drinks and the form of service can change, so they should be checked directly before the visit.

### Casa da Glória Restaurante

Casa da Glória is located on the Quadrado itself and has its own context in local history and architecture. Its gastronomic operation should be considered separately from the lodging.

It can be an option for dinner and drinks in a historic house, according to the current operation. Service to the public, menu and prices should be checked directly.

### Santo Café

Santo Café works as a coffee shop and bakery on the Quadrado. Coffees, breads, cakes and snacks form the center of its proposal.

It is useful for those looking for breakfast or an afternoon coffee, according to the current hours, as well as a break during the walk or a lighter meal than a full dinner.

Hours and available items should be confirmed directly.

### Bacio di Latte

Bacio di Latte is a reference for gelato and coffee in the Quadrado area, not a dinner restaurant.

The proposal is geared to ice cream, desserts, coffees and breaks during the walk. The specific location, hours and current operation should be verified before the visit.

### Tapioca da Elma

Tapioca da Elma represents a simple and traditional experience at the entrance or area of the Quadrado.

The sweet and savory tapiocas can work as a snack or informal meal, without the service format of the dinner restaurants. It is also an alternative for those who want to spend less, although this guide does not indicate fixed prices.

## How to choose according to the occasion

### For dinner for two

The Quadrado offers different ideas for a romantic dinner, without that meaning necessarily quiet environments.

- **O Cacau:** Bahian cuisine and vegetation around the tables.
- **Capim Santo:** garden, history and creative Brazilian cuisine.
- **Favoritto:** Mediterranean cuisine and seafood.
- **El Gordo:** special occasion and the possibility of a view, depending on the area.
- **Flô:** contemporary proposal.
- **UXUA:** sophisticated experience integrated into the historic context.

The movement of the square can change considerably according to the day and the season.

### For families

Families usually benefit from varied menus, shareable dishes and less formal environments.

Rabanete can work for lunch; Silvana & Cia, for Bahian food; Gino and Maritaca, for pasta and pizzas; and Allegria, when its different operations are available.

This does not mean that all of them offer high chairs, changing rooms, a children's area or a kids' menu. These services should be confirmed directly.

### For groups

Allegria, Bonitto, Favoritto, Gino, Maritaca and Silvana & Cia are options to consider for their variety or sharing potential.

Table capacity and service for groups need to be verified in advance. In high season, arriving without a reservation can limit the choices.

### For eating on a budget

The Quadrado brings together everything from tapiocas and cafés to sophisticated restaurants. For a more economical meal, the most suitable profiles are usually:

- Rabanete, especially at lunch and according to the current format;
- Santo Café, for coffee, a snack or a light meal;
- Tapioca da Elma, for a quick and informal choice;
- pizzerias and pasta houses, when dishes can be shared.

Value for money is not just the lowest price. Portion sizes, drinks, starters, desserts and the possibility of splitting dishes interfere in the bill.

## How much does it cost to eat on the Quadrado?

There is no truly representative average price for the whole Quadrado. A tapioca, a coffee and a dinner with starter, wine and dessert belong to very different categories.

As a conceptual reference, the options can be understood like this:

- **Coffee, tapioca or snack:** quick and light meals;
- **Casual lunch:** buffet, by weight or simple dishes;
- **Intermediate dinner:** table service and a more elaborate menu;
- **Sophisticated dinner:** experiences geared to special occasions.

The restaurants do not receive fixed price symbols in this guide because menus and prices change. Drinks, couvert, desserts and shared dishes also change the total considerably.

## Reservations, hours and the Quadrado at night

It is not always necessary to book, but planning reduces the risk of not finding a table at the desired restaurant.

![Trancoso's Quadrado at dusk](/img/guia/int-restaurantes-quadrado.webp)

A reservation deserves special attention at:

- New Year's Eve;
- January;
- Carnival;
- school holidays;
- extended public holidays;
- busy weekends;
- groups;
- birthdays and special occasions.

There is no universal deadline. Demand depends on the season, the size of the group and the restaurant.

It is also worth confirming:

- whether the house will open on the desired date;
- the kitchen hours;
- whether there are outdoor tables;
- the reservation and cancellation policy;
- the existence of special menus;
- changes of operation in the low season.

Arriving at the Quadrado and choosing on the spot can work in quiet periods. On busy dates, this strategy offers less security, especially for those who prefer a specific house or table.

## Conclusion

The restaurants on Trancoso's Quadrado reflect different layers of the village: Bahian tradition, the hidden gardens, the old little houses, contemporary cuisine and the evening movement.

Choosing well depends less on looking for an absolute winner and more on matching cuisine, time, budget and occasion. It is also important to confirm menus, reservations and opening, because Trancoso changes pace throughout the year.

With that care, the Quadrado allows you to organize everything from a casual lunch to a celebration dinner without losing touch with the historic setting that made the region so renowned.
