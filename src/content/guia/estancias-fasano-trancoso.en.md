# Estâncias Fasano in Trancoso: what the residential experience is like

The proposal combines the autonomy of a residence with an experience integrated into the complex.

Estâncias Fasano should be treated as its own residential area, linked to Fasano, and not as part of Itapororoca. House configuration, capacity, staff and included services can vary according to the property and the booking modality.

## What Estâncias Fasano are

The terms Hotel, Villas, Residences and Estâncias should not automatically be treated as equivalent. The commercial nomenclature and the official difference between these modalities need to be verified in the presentation of each property.

In practical terms, the Fasano Hotel follows the dynamics of hotel accommodation. The Estâncias, in turn, are associated with a residential experience, with social areas, a more independent routine and the capacity to receive families or groups.

All houses at Estâncias Fasano have access to the Fasano complex. Guests can use areas such as the gym, sauna and pool, besides the other applicable facilities.

This does not mean that all residences have the same configuration or offer the same services inside the house. Before booking, it is important to confirm:

- the unit's official classification;
- the exact location;
- the capacity and the distribution of the bedrooms;
- the composition of the house staff;
- the services included or hired separately;
- the conditions applicable to the use of the complex's facilities.

## What the accommodation experience is like

### House routine

Estâncias Fasano tend to make more sense for those who value using the house during the trip, and not just as a place to sleep. Social areas and a residential routine can make longer stays, family trips and groups that prefer to stay together easier.

This dynamic is different from accommodation in separate rooms or suites. At the same time, size, style, privacy and structure should not be standardized: these characteristics depend on the chosen residence.

### Nature, privacy and property configuration

The experience at Estâncias Fasano is linked to very high-standard houses and access to the Fasano complex. Elements such as architecture, gardens, leisure areas, position on the plot and the visual relationship with the landscape should be assessed property by property.

Characteristics such as a sea view also need to be checked in the description of the chosen unit. Beach access through the Fasano complex, on the other hand, applies to all houses in the Estâncias.

## Services linked to Fasano

Access to the Fasano complex is a relevant extension of the stay at the Estâncias. All houses offer guests access to the complex, including gym, sauna, pool, beach access and other applicable facilities.

The composition of services inside each residence, however, can vary. Some houses may have their own staff, a chef or cook and support professionals. Additional services can also be organized or hired separately, according to the property and availability.

Before booking, it is worth asking about:

## Estâncias Fasano or a house in Itapororoca?

Estâncias Fasano and Itapororoca should be considered distinct areas. The choice depends less on the property's name and more on the desired experience.

The Estâncias can come onto the radar of those looking for a very high-standard residence linked to the Fasano complex. Access to the complex's facilities is an important part of the proposal, while staff, meals and other house services vary according to the property.

Itapororoca, in turn, is a high-standard area with larger houses, nature and good privacy. There are beachfront options and properties with a sea view. In Altos de Itapororoca, the houses have beach access.

Before deciding, it is worth comparing:

- the house's location;
- the effective relationship with the beach;
- the need for transportation;
- the capacity and bedroom configuration;
- the staff and included services;
- access to the complex's facilities;
- the rules of the property and the shared areas.

Those considering other areas of Trancoso should also note that the routine outside the Quadrado's surroundings can require different logistics. Journeys need to be assessed according to the house's exact location and the planned itinerary.

## Who this accommodation makes sense for

### Families and groups

The residential experience can work well for families, groups of friends and trips with different generations. Staying in the same house makes meals, gatherings and socializing easier, but capacity, accessibility and suite distribution need to be checked for each property.

Children's items, ground-floor bedrooms, adapted bathrooms and circulation conditions should also not be presumed. These characteristics need to be verified house by house.

### Those who value autonomy

The residences interest those who prefer to organize their own schedules and have more space to spend part of the day at the property. This profile usually enjoys the house better than the traveler who intends to stay out for the entire stay.

### Those who want to use the Fasano complex

All houses at Estâncias Fasano offer access to the complex. Guests can use the gym, sauna, pool, beach access and other applicable facilities, making this integration one of the accommodation's main differentials.

Services provided inside the residence, on the other hand, depend on the property. The composition of the staff and any additional hires should be confirmed before booking.
